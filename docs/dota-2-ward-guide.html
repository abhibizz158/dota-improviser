Questions

Where should supports ward early?

Start with the river runes and your lane’s entrances. The guide lists spots by side and phase.

Is the vision exact?

Trees come from Dota’s map file. Cliff height is estimated, so treat edges as approximate.

Does it show enemy wards during a game?

No. Nothing hidden is shown while you play.
