Questions

Which matches can be reviewed?

Finished matches. Detailed views need OpenDota’s parsed replay.

Why is my history empty?

Turn on “Expose Public Match Data” in Dota’s settings.

Can I review a match while it is live?

No. Review and replay are for finished matches only.
