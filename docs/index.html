What it does

Help before, during and after a match

1

While you pick heroes

See which heroes do well against the enemy team, and why, in plain words.

  • Three suggestions that update as heroes are revealed
  • Matched to the lane you play and the heroes you know
  • Tells you what your team is still missing
2

While you play

A small panel sits on top of the game with your own numbers and reminders. It never plays for you.

  • What item to buy next, and when you can afford it
  • Reminders for runes and other timings
  • For supports: a map showing good places for wards
3

After the match

Find out what went well and what to fix next time, without reading spreadsheets.

  • Three clear takeaways
  • A map you can scrub through, minute by minute
  • Whether your key items came early or late

Plan with your team

One map, three ways to learn

Draw a plan, replay a past match, or watch every hero move. Friends can join with a room code and a PIN.

Place heroes and wards, draw arrows, and step through your plan like moves in chess.

Replay a finished match from its record: fights, wards and towers, in order. No download needed.

Load the real replay and watch all ten heroes move, with what each team could see.

Safety and fair play

Your computer, your data

A plain list of what the app does and does not do with your computer and your information.

Stays on your computer

Your match history and settings are saved on your own computer. The developer runs no server and receives none of it.

No password needed

The app never asks for your Steam password or any payment details.

No ads or tracking

No adverts, no analytics, no selling of data, and no extra programs bundled in the installer.

What leaves your computer, and when
  • Always: the app downloads public game statistics and updates from OpenDota, Valve and GitHub.
  • If you link your Steam account: your account number is sent to OpenDota to look up your public matches.
  • If you use the AI helper or chat translation: your question, game details or chat lines go to your own Puter account when you ask. A screenshot of your death screen is sent only if you switch that option on and press the button.
  • If you open a team room: the plan, your cursor, Steam name, picture and account number are shared with the people who join. Connection services (PeerJS and Google) help the computers find each other, and a room member can see your local network address.

About viruses and security warnings

We add no harmful or hidden software. Installers are built automatically on GitHub, and each release lists a checksum so you can confirm your file was not changed.

They are not signed with a paid certificate and have not been certified by an outside security company, so Windows and macOS show a warning. If you are unsure, scan the file with your antivirus or at virustotal.com before opening it. Never switch your antivirus off to install it: tell us instead.

Fair play: it only knows what you can already see

What it uses

  • Information Dota itself shares about your own game, once you switch that on
  • Public statistics anyone can look up
  • What you type in, and a few game files already on your computer
  • Optional and off by default: reading hero pictures on your screen during hero picks, and one screenshot when your hero dies

What it never does

  • Look inside the game’s memory or network traffic
  • Click or type in Dota for you
  • Show where enemies are, or anything else the game hides
  • Show ads, sell your data or ask for money

How to install

About three minutes

  1. Download

    Download the Windows installer. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose to keep it.

  2. Open the file

    If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Install

    Keep or change the folder, then finish. It installs for your own user account, so no administrator password is needed.

  4. Open the app

    A short setup guide appears. Every step can be skipped and reopened later from Settings.

Permissions on Windows

  • Nothing special to allow. The app works with normal user permissions.
  • Dota display mode: in Dota’s video settings choose Borderless Window, so the in-game panel can appear on top.
  • If Windows Firewall asks while you use a team room, allow it on private networks.

Why the warning? This free app does not have a paid publisher certificate, so Windows cannot name who made it. Only download it from this page or the official GitHub releases.

  1. Check your Mac

    Apple menu, then About This Mac. The chip should say Apple M1 or newer, on macOS 13 or later.

  2. Download and open

    Download the Mac installer, then double-click it. If your Mac says it cannot check it, click Done. Do not move it to the Bin.

  3. Allow the installer

    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway.

  4. Install and open

    Follow the installer and enter your Mac password when asked. Then open Dota Improviser from Applications.

What your Mac may ask you to allow

  • Documents folder Needed
    If asked, click Allow. The app keeps its own data in a folder there.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording Only for two optional features
    Reading hero pictures during picks, and the death screenshot. If you switch one on: open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Dota Improviser, then quit and reopen the app. Everything else works without it. The app records no sound.
  • After an app update If you use those features
    Your Mac forgets the screen permission. Turn it on again in the same place and reopen the app.
  • Keychain Only for the AI helper
    If asked after you sign in, click Allow. It keeps your sign-in safe on your Mac.

Why the warning? This free app has not been checked (“notarized”) by Apple. Only download it from this page or the official GitHub releases.

Last step on both: connect Dota

  1. In the app

    In the setup guide, choose to connect the game feed. The app adds one small settings file to Dota.

  2. In Steam

    Right-click Dota 2, choose Properties, and paste -gamestateintegration into Launch Options.

  3. Restart Dota

    Start a practice match. The app shows GSI live when it is connected.

Extra: check that your download is genuine

Each release includes a file called SHA256SUMS.txt. Compare its code with your file’s code.

Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash .\Dota-Improviser-*-windows-x64-setup.exe

Mac (Terminal): shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Dota-Improviser-*-macos-arm64.pkg

    Questions

    Quick answers

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No ads, no paid version, nothing to unlock.

    Could it get my account banned?

    It only uses what you can already see, never reads the game’s memory and never clicks or types in Dota. It is a fan project that Valve has not approved or endorsed, so nobody can promise anything on Valve’s behalf.

    Do I need to sign up?

    No. Linking your Steam account is optional and lets the app learn from your public match history. For that, turn on “Expose Public Match Data” in Dota’s settings. The AI assistant is optional and uses a Puter account.

    Where is my information kept?

    On your own computer, in a folder inside Documents. You can move or delete it from the app’s settings.

    Which computers does it work on?

    Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), and Macs with an Apple chip on macOS 13 or later. Not on Intel Macs, Linux, phones or tablets.

    How do updates work?

    The app checks for new versions itself and asks before installing. You can also download the newest one from this page.

    How do I remove it?

    In the app: Settings, then Storage and uninstall.
