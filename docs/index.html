What it does

Help before, during and after a match

1

While you pick heroes

See which heroes do well against the enemy team, and why, in plain words.

  • Three suggestions that update as heroes are revealed
  • Matched to the lane you play and the heroes you know
  • Tells you what your team is still missing
2

While you play

A small panel sits on top of the game with your own numbers and reminders. It never plays for you.

  • What item to buy next, and when you can afford it
  • Reminders for runes and other timings
  • For supports: a map showing good places for wards
3

After the match

Find out what went well and what to fix next time, without reading spreadsheets.

  • Three clear takeaways
  • A map you can scrub through, minute by minute
  • Whether your key items came early or late

Plan with your team

One map, three ways to learn

Draw a plan, replay a past match, or watch every hero move. Friends can join with a room code and a PIN.

Place heroes and wards, draw arrows, and step through your plan like moves in chess.

Replay a finished match from its record: fights, wards and towers, in order. No download needed.

Load the real replay and watch all ten heroes move, with what each team could see.

Fair play

It only knows what you can already see

What it uses

  • Information Dota itself shares about your own hero, once you switch that on
  • Public statistics anyone can look up
  • What you type in
  • Optional and off by default: reading hero pictures on your screen during hero picks only

What it never does

  • Look inside the game’s memory or network traffic
  • Click or type in Dota for you
  • Show where enemies are, or anything else the game hides
  • Show ads, sell your data or ask for money

How to install

About three minutes

  1. Download

    Download the Windows installer. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose to keep it.

  2. Open it

    If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Follow the setup

    Open the app. It walks you through connecting Dota. Every step can be skipped.

Why the warning? This free app does not have a paid publisher certificate, so Windows cannot name who made it. Only download it from this page or the official GitHub releases.

For the in-game panel to show, set Dota’s display mode to Borderless Window.

  1. Check your Mac

    Apple menu, then About This Mac. The chip should say Apple M1 or newer, on macOS 13 or later.

  2. Download and open

    Download the Mac installer and double-click it.

  3. If your Mac blocks it

    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Then follow the app’s setup.

Why the warning? This free app has not been checked (“notarized”) by Apple. Only download it from this page or the official GitHub releases.

Extra: check that your download is genuine

Each release includes a file called SHA256SUMS.txt. Compare its code with your file’s code.

Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash .\Dota-Improviser-*-windows-x64-setup.exe

Mac (Terminal): shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Dota-Improviser-*-macos-arm64.pkg

    Questions

    Quick answers

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No ads, no paid version, nothing to unlock.

    Could it get my account banned?

    It only uses what you can already see, never reads the game’s memory and never clicks or types in Dota. It is a fan project that Valve has not approved or endorsed, so nobody can promise anything on Valve’s behalf.

    Do I need to sign up?

    No. Linking your Steam account is optional and lets the app learn from your public match history. For that, turn on “Expose Public Match Data” in Dota’s settings. The AI assistant is optional and uses a Puter account.

    Where is my information kept?

    On your own computer, in a folder inside Documents. You can move or delete it from the app’s settings.

    Which computers does it work on?

    Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), and Macs with an Apple chip on macOS 13 or later. Not on Intel Macs, Linux, phones or tablets.

    How do updates work?

    The app checks for new versions itself and asks before installing. You can also download the newest one from this page.

    How do I remove it?

    In the app: Settings, then Storage and uninstall.
