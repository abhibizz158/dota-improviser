How it works

Connect once

The app uses Dota’s Game State Integration, which shares information about your own game.

Play

The panel shows your numbers, the next item and reminders. It never clicks or types for you.

Hide or resize

Ctrl or ⌘ + Shift + H hides it. Ctrl or ⌘ + Shift + C switches between compact and large.

Questions

Is this Dota 2 overlay free?

Yes. No ads and no paid version.

Does the overlay work on Mac?

Yes, on Apple chip Macs with macOS 13 or later, and on Windows 10 or 11.

Does it show enemy information?

No. It never shows enemy positions or anything the game hides.
