Questions

How are counter picks chosen?

From public matchup statistics, your lane, what your team is missing and your own record on each hero.

Does it work for Single Draft or Ability Draft?

Yes. It ranks the heroes Single Draft offers you and sorts the Ability Draft ability pool.

Does it pick for me?

No. It suggests, and you pick.
