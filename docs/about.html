About Dota Improviser

Dota Improviser is a free, fan-made desktop app that helps you pick, play and review Dota 2 matches. This is its official website.

What it does

Draft helper

Suggests picks from counters, your lane, your team’s gaps and the heroes you play well.

In-game overlay

Your own stats, item suggestions, rune and stack timers and a support view.

Match review

Takeaways, item timings and replays of finished matches on a map.

Wards and strategy

Ward spots with what they see, and a board to plan with teammates.

What it does not do

It never reads game memory or network traffic, never clicks or types in Dota, and never shows enemy positions or anything the game hides. Safety and privacy.
