Self-learning

It learns how you play

The more you play, the more the advice is about you. It starts with your first matches and the learning happens on your own computer.

What it learns

Your heroes

Which heroes and lanes you do well on. This counts in draft suggestions from the first game.

Your items

Which items went with your wins and losses on a hero. After 3 games with purchase records it also shows your usual build.

Lineups like this

Once 3 of your past games had similar enemies, it shows how you did against them.

Your lane habits

Lose the lane early in half your recent reviewed games on a hero (3 or more), and its lane tips start there. Late first items work the same way.

Your wards

From saved replays: where your wards lasted, where they were found and where enemies place theirs.

Chat phrases

Short translations and your corrections are remembered, so repeats need no AI.

How it stays sensible

Little data, little change

One or two games only nudge the advice. Around 10 to 15 move it properly.

Newer counts more

A match counts half after about four months. Old patches count less, most of all for heroes that were reworked.

Modes kept apart

Turbo numbers never inflate your ranked averages, and Ability Draft does not teach hero comfort.

Switch it on

  1. 1. Link SteamAdd your Dota account in the app’s setup guide.
  2. 2. Share matchesIn Dota 2 turn on “Expose Public Match Data”, and set your Steam profile’s game details to public. Prefer not to? Turn on “Save my completed match stats locally” in Live Coach: with the game feed connected it keeps up to 20 of your own scoreboards, from then on.
  3. 3. Open reviewsOpen finished matches in Match Review. Item, lane and ward learning needs matches OpenDota has parsed.
  4. 4. Keep playingNothing to train by hand. Your profile is rebuilt from what is saved.

Questions

Does Dota Improviser learn from my games?

Yes. It builds a profile from your own matches and uses it for draft suggestions, item suggestions, tips and ward spots.

Is my learning shared with other players?

No. It stays on your computer and is never mixed with anyone else’s.

Is it a large AI model?

No. The learning is simple statistics that run on your computer. The optional AI assistant is separate and uses your own Puter account.

Why is nothing being learned?

There are no matches to learn from yet. Check both public data settings, or turn on local saving, then refresh your history.

Does it learn about Dota patches?

It refreshes hero and item data after a patch and gives older matches less weight.

Can I erase it?

Yes. Clear local data in the app’s settings removes your account, saved matches and preferences.
