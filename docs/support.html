Common questions

What is Dota Improviser?

A free desktop companion app for Dota 2 on Windows and Apple Silicon Macs. It suggests heroes in the draft, shows a live overlay, reviews matches and has a team strategy board.

Is it an official Valve or Dota 2 product?

No. It is a fan-made project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve Corporation.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no paid version, nothing to unlock.

Could my account get banned?

It only uses what you can already see, never reads game memory and never clicks or types in Dota. Valve has not approved it, so nobody can promise anything on Valve’s behalf.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Linking Steam is optional; turn on “Expose Public Match Data” in Dota for match history. The AI helper is optional and uses a Puter account.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and Apple chip Macs on macOS 13 or later. Not Intel Macs, Linux or phones.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions and asks before installing.

Can I copy, sell or modify it?

No. It is free for personal use only. Copying, selling, reverse engineering and AI extraction need written permission. See the terms of use.

How do I remove it?

In the app: Settings, then Storage and uninstall.
